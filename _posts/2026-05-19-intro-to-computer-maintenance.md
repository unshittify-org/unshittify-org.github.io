Think of this series as "Computer Maintenance for Dummies".

You want to do some of the cool stuff we're talking about on this blog. You don't have the skills to do it yet. But you can follow instructions damn well. You just need us to tell you *what* to do and *why* you're doing it.

That's what this series is for.

<!--more-->

These will be relatively short posts, about focused elements of computer maintenance.

The other purpose of these posts is to be a reference - for example, there are lots of reasons you may want to check something out in the BIOS.  Rather than give instructions for accessing the BIOS in every post that needs it, we'll make a single post with those instructions in this series, and link to it anywhere you need to do that.

The skills in this series are the building blocks of computer maintenance.  We want you to be able to refer back to them whenever you need them, in whatever context you need them.
